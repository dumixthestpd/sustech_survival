## Access
- **On campus**: IP auto-recognized
- **Off campus**: VPN or `Collections → Campus Access` with card account

### Institutional / SSO Login (Off-Campus)

When accessing databases from off-campus, look for:
- **"Login via institution"** or **"Institution login"** (JSTOR, SAGE, Wiley, etc.)
- **"Access through your institution / SSO"** (Taylor & Francis, Springer)
- **"Shibboleth login"** (Elsevier/ScienceDirect, IEEE)

**Steps:**
1. Click the institutional login button on the database
2. Search for **"Southern University of Science and Technology"** or **"SUSTech"**
3. Redirected to SUSTech CAS → log in with student account
4. Full institutional access granted

**Direct database links (SUSTech Shibboleth):**
- SAGE Journals: `https://journals.sagepub.com` → Login via institution
- JSTOR: `https://www.jstor.org` → Institution login
- Wiley: `https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com` → Access through your institution
- Taylor & Francis: `https://www.tandfonline.com` → Login via Shibboleth
- IEEE: `https://ieeexplore.ieee.org` → Institutional login

**Library off-campus page:** `https://lib.sustech.edu.cn` → 校外访问

## Search Tips
- Use **Primo** (library homepage) for cross-database discovery
- Use **Web of Science Advanced Search** for structured field queries: `TS=`, `TI=`, `AU=`
- Use **ProQuest Advanced Search** for field-specific: `title()`, `abs()`, `fulltext()`

## Common Field Codes (WoS)
- `TS` = Topic (title, abstract, keywords)
- `TI` = Title
- `AU` = Author
- `SO` = Source (journal)
- `PY` = Publication year
